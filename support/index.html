Finding and pairing

Which TVs work?

TVs and streaming boxes running Android TV or Google TV with the Android TV Remote Service, for example Sony BRAVIA, TCL, Hisense, Philips and Chromecast with Google TV.

How do I pair my TV?

Tap the TV name at the top of the remote and choose Connect next to your TV. A six-character code appears on the TV; type it in and tap Submit. You only do this once per TV.

My TV isn't listed

Switch the TV on and make sure it and your iPhone are on the same Wi-Fi network (not a guest network, which often blocks devices from seeing each other). Some TVs stop answering in deep standby; wake them with their own remote first.

Local Network permission

Couchpad needs Local Network access to find and talk to your TV. If you declined it, turn it on in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network › Couchpad.

The code on the TV disappeared, or I mistyped it

Tap "No code on the TV? Show a new one" to start again. A mistyped code is caught right away; just type it again.

Using the remote

The remote says "Not connected"

Couchpad keeps retrying while the remote is open. Check that the TV is on and on the same Wi-Fi. If the TV was reset, Couchpad asks you to pair again.

Typing with the keyboard

Open a search or text box on the TV first, then tap the keyboard key. Letters appear on the TV as you type; Return sends Enter.

The mic key

It opens the TV's own voice search, using the TV remote's or TV's microphone. Couchpad never uses your iPhone's microphone.

App shortcuts

Tap an app icon on the remote to open it on the TV. Settings › Manage applications lets you reorder, remove and add apps. The app must be installed on the TV.

Holding a key repeats too fast or not at all

Settings › Remote behavior › Smooth Press turns hold-to-repeat on or off for volume, channels and arrows.

Is my data private?

Yes. Couchpad talks only to TVs on your Wi-Fi; the only thing it sends to the internet is an anonymous crash report if the app crashes. See the Privacy Policy.

Reporting a problem

Please tell us your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings › Contact Support adds them for you), your TV model, what you did and what happened instead. A screenshot or screen recording helps.

Ideas and feature requests

Suggest features and vote on others' ideas on the Couchpad feature board (also in Settings › Feature Requests).
